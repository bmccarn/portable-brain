# Crib placement

## Context
The room has one east window with corded blinds and a heat vent on the north wall. The owner's first instinct, captured in `inbox/` on 2026-08-10, was "not under the window"; the pediatrician's handout says the same ([Pediatrician safe-sleep handout](../sources/pediatrician-safe-sleep-handout.md)).

## Decision
West wall, centered, at least an arm's length from anything with a cord. The glider takes the window.

## Consequences
The dresser moves to the north wall by the door and the bookshelf to the south wall. Daylight falls on the glider, not the crib, which also helps naps.

## Alternatives considered
- Under the window: the best view, the worst for cords and drafts — rejected on the handout's rule.
- North wall: over the heat vent — rejected; the vent would blow on the crib all winter.
- South wall by the door: workable, but every entry at night passes the crib — rejected for sleep disruption.
