# Paint color

## Context
The owner wanted a calm, non-themed room and needed to paint early enough to air it out. The swatch is preserved in [Paint swatch photo — sage](../sources/paint-swatch-sage.md); the sample patch on the east wall was checked in morning and evening light.

## Decision
"Sage Meadow", eggshell, zero-VOC, two coats on all four walls; trim stays white.

## Consequences
Painting could happen in August (done 2026-08-20 and 21) with weeks to cure. Textiles stay white and yellow so the room is not green on green.

## Alternatives considered
- Pale yellow: warm, but it clashed with the yellow quilt.
- Light grey: calm on the card, but the room faces east and the sample went cold in morning light.
- A wallpaper accent wall: rejected for cost and for the adhesive's off-gassing.
