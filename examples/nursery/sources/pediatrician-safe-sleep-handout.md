## Source material
> Baby sleeps on their back, every sleep, until their first birthday.
> Firm, flat mattress with only a fitted sheet. No pillows, bumpers, blankets, or soft toys in the crib.
> A crib, bassinet, or play yard that meets current safety standards; nothing older that may have been recalled.
> Keep the sleep space in the parents' room for the first six months if possible.
> Keep the crib away from windows, blind cords, heaters, and anything the baby could pull in.
> Room temperature comfortable for a lightly clothed adult; dress the baby in one more layer than you wear.
> Offer a pacifier at sleep time once feeding is established.
> No smoke exposure in the home or car.
