## Source material
Extracted from the PDF (twelve steps; the ones that matter later):

- Step 3: the headboard and footboard bolt to the side rails with the eight long bolts; hand-tighten all eight before the final pass.
- Step 7: the mattress support has three heights. Use the highest only for a newborn; lower it as soon as the baby can push up on hands and knees; lowest position once they can pull to stand.
- Step 10: re-tighten every bolt after the first week of use and monthly after that.
- Conversion to a toddler bed needs the separate conversion kit, which is not included.
