# Safe sleep

## Summary
The rules the room follows, as written on the pediatrician's handout and kept verbatim in [Pediatrician safe-sleep handout](../sources/pediatrician-safe-sleep-handout.md): back to sleep for every sleep; a firm, flat mattress with only a fitted sheet; nothing soft in the crib; a crib that meets current standards; the crib away from windows, cords, and heat; room-sharing for the first months; a comfortable room temperature.

## Notes
What this changed in the setup: the crib's position ([Layout](layout.md)); buying a new crib rather than using the one in the attic ([Crib](inventory/crib.md)); the quilt going on the wall instead of in the crib ([Paint and color](paint-and-color.md)); and a bassinet for the parents' room for the first months, which is outside this room's scope and sits as a follow-up on [Nursery setup](../work/nursery-setup.md).
