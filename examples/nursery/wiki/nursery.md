# Nursery

## Summary
The small bedroom at the end of the hall — about 10 by 11 feet, one east-facing window, a heat vent on the north wall, door on the south wall — is becoming the nursery. The due date is mid-October 2026 and the aim is a usable room by week 36. As of 2026-08-22 the walls are painted and the crib is assembled; what remains is curtains, outlet covers, anchoring, and linens, tracked in [Nursery setup](../work/nursery-setup.md).

## Notes
Three principles, in priority order:

1. **Safe sleep first.** Everything about the crib and its position follows [Safe sleep](safe-sleep.md).
2. **Calm, not themed.** Sage walls, white trim, wood furniture; see [Paint and color](paint-and-color.md).
3. **One-handed at 3 a.m.** Anything needed at night is reachable from the glider or the changing table without putting the baby down; see [Layout](layout.md).
