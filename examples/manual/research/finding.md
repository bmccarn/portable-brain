# Morning reminders improve response rate

## Finding
Participants replied more often when reminders arrived before 10:00.

## Evidence
The pilot recorded 18 replies from 24 morning reminders and 9 from 24 afternoon reminders.

## Interpretation
Morning reminders may reach participants before competing priorities accumulate.

## Limitations
The pilot involved one team over two weeks.
